----- The deltatime struggle...
Accurate deltatime (Jonas Tyroller)
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yGhfUcPjXuE
+
Clamping my dt value to a maximum value

I naively thought that this would be the solution to all my problems...
But when playtesting the game at lower frame rates I noticed that my player
character was jumping at different heights when running at lower FPS! There is
an issue with this approach ;(

With some more research I found this fantastic resource:

https://gafferongames.com/post/fix_your_timestep/

The "Fix Your Timestep!" article is great as it walks through different
implementations and points out the positives and negatives.

I like the sound of the "Semi-fixed timestep" with clamping/slowdown to avoid
the dreaded "spiral of death".

"The final touch" sounds very promising until you realise you have to store a
copy of all your game state to interpolate between... amongst other issues
outlined in this article:

https://web.archive.org/web/20220522084249/https://www.gamedeveloper.com/programming/fixing-your-time-step-the-easy-way-with-the-golden-4-8537-ms-

I didn't like the approach in the above article but the author pointed out some
shortcomings with the approach Glenn Fiedler discussed.

Another article following "The final touch" approach discussed by Fiedler.

https://gamesfromwithin.com/casey-and-the-clearly-deterministic-contraptions

Ultimately, fixed delta time is both simple, and reproducable. To save any more
headaches in the short-term I will be using a fixed delta time. I chose 60FPS
because it is common and a good FPS for web builds in my experience.
